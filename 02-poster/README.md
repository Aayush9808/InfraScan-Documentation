# Poster preparation

The supplied `GLBITM POSTER Format.pptx` is a one-page poster with institutional header and sections for Group ID, Abstract, Introduction, Objectives & Motivation, Methodology, Experimentation & Results, Conclusion, References, and Workflow Diagram.

- [Poster copy](poster-content.md)
- [Layout guide](poster-layout-guide.md)

Keep the poster concise. Put equations, limitations, and licensing details in the report or speaker notes.
