# Poster layout guide

Follow the supplied GLBITM poster rather than replacing its institutional identity.

- Keep the title and department header in the template's top band.
- Use a three-column body: abstract/introduction; methodology/workflow; results/conclusion/references.
- Reserve the largest visual region for the workflow diagram.
- Use one readable sans-serif family and a restrained cyan/navy accent consistent with InfraScan.
- Keep body text short enough to read at print distance; target 45–70 words per section.
- Use diagram labels of at least 18–22 pt in the final poster export.
- Add screenshots only after capturing them from a real run and labeling the model/input conditions.
- Do not include the excluded dataset or checkpoint in the poster as if redistributed.
