# Problem statement and objectives

## Problem

Manual visual inspection can be time-consuming and inconsistent. A computer-vision assistant may help screen road imagery, but its usefulness depends on representative data, a valid checkpoint, threshold calibration, and human review.

## Objectives

1. Detect pothole regions using bounding boxes.
2. Accept practical visual inputs supported by the source implementation.
3. Provide annotated output and structured telemetry where implemented.
4. Make missing assets and invalid inputs explicit.
5. Document provenance, testing status, and limitations honestly.

## Non-goals

- No claim of autonomous road maintenance decisions.
- No claim of universal geographic generalization.
- No claim of fresh accuracy without a reproducible evaluation.
- No claim that excluded assets may be redistributed.
