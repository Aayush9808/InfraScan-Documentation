# Testing and limitations questions

1. What is a smoke test? Minimal startup/function check.
2. What is blocked? A test requiring unavailable assets.
3. Why test syntax? Catch parse errors before runtime.
4. Why scan secrets? Prevent accidental disclosure.
5. Why scan absolute paths? Avoid machine-specific configuration.
6. What is a false positive? Incorrect detection on a non-pothole.
7. What is a false negative? Missed pothole.
8. Why can camera tests differ? Hardware and permissions.
9. What would improve confidence? Authorized data, fresh evaluation, and repeatable evidence.
10. What is the most important limitation? Results depend on training data and checkpoint validity.
